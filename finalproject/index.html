About Me

My name is David Adepegba. I am a Data Analyst who enjoys working with data, technology, and web development. With over five years of experience in data analysis and business reporting, I have primarily worked in data cleaning, dashboard development, database management, performance monitoring, and reporting automation. My experience spans both technical data roles and operational support functions, where I have focused on improving data accuracy, efficiency, and decision-making processes.

Since May 2023, I have been working at Joshmit Technologies in Lagos as a Data Analyst, SQL data extraction, and automated reporting solutions. In this role, I design and deploy Power BI and Tableau dashboards, optimise SQL queries, and conduct root-cause analyses that improve operational efficiency and reporting speed.

From August 2020 to May 2023, I worked at Trustline Logistics in Lagos as an Administrative Assistant with strong data responsibilities. During this period, I digitised logistics databases, conducted KPI audits, built Excel performance dashboards, and supported inventory documentation using Power Query and advanced Excel formulas. My work helped improve record retrieval efficiency and reduce processing errors.

Throughout my career, I have focused on transforming raw data into actionable insights while maintaining strong attention to detail, confidentiality, and reporting accuracy. I place strong emphasis on structured data management, automation, and clear data storytelling to support informed business decisions.
